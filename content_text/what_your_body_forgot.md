# What Your Body Forgot
### The Rotten Fencepost Package

Why diets fail, and what a Paniolo learned about eating the way God made you.

The weight comes back.
The energy goes again.
You start over on Monday. Again.

Maybe it was never your willpower.

When a fence keeps leaning, a good rancher doesn't just keep pushing it straight. He walks the line and finds the post that's rotten underneath. Fix that, and the fence stands.

Your body works the same way. If the same problem keeps coming back, something underneath hasn't been found yet.

## Who I am

Aloha. I'm Phil Stephens, a Native Hawaiian Paniolo and pastor on Molokaʻi. I've spent thirty years on ranches on Molokaʻi, Maui, the Big Island and in Colorado, watching how animals eat when nobody tells them when to. I've lived among Native peoples and seen how they ate before the modern plate came along.

This book is what I found.
